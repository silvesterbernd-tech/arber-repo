## 1. Profile for 4318161 "Marimanga" (Kosovar band — keep this page)

Albanian rock band from Kosovo, led by guitarist and singer Njazi Bytyqi
(1946/1947 – 3 March 2018, Prizren). One of the first rock groups in Kosovo,
appearing on the scene around 1970 per Gëzim Krasniqi's study of the Albanian
rock scene in Yugoslavia; a Facebook page connected to Bytyqi dates the group
to 1968, so the formation year is not settled.

The band's best-known recording is "Vogelushja Keti" ("Petite Keti"),
recorded in 1970 in the studios of TVP (Televizioni i Prishtinës), the
Pristina television station. Session footage survives on the RTP Arkiv
YouTube channel ("Njazi Bytyqi Grupi Marimanga – Arkiv 1970 TVP"); this
appears to be the only surviving trace of the recording.

Bytyqi is described in his koha.net obituary (5 March 2018) as "the
initiator of pop-rock music in Kosovo"; bassist Sheki Hoxha called him "the
first of the first in Kosovo's pop & rock 'n' roll." Bytyqi made what is
believed to be the first Albanian-language music video, filmed in Prizren in
the 1970s and broadcast on Croatian television. He withdrew from public life
after the 1970s but continued playing guitar until his death in 2018. His
sons Elvis and Gjulian Bytyqi, both guitarists, preserved his archive; Elvis
released the double LP "Nijazi Bytyqi" in 2024.

Lineup per family captions (1968): Njazi Bytyqi (vocals/guitar), Zoran
Milosavljevic (lead guitar), Ljubisa Patenkovic (bass), Vladimir Nikolic
(drums). Full lineup of the 1970 TVP session not documented — do not invent
names.

NOT to be confused with Marimanga Trio, a separate Sarajevo-based jazz group
active in the 2000s (see artist "Marimanga Trio").

## 2. NEW PAGE — "Marimanga Trio" (Bosnian jazz group)

Sarajevo-based jazz group active in the 2000s. Released the album
"Adventure Has Just Begun" (2000) and collaborated with Bojan Zulfikarpašić
("Marimanga Trio & Bojan Zulfikarpašić", 2004); also appears backing Mujo
Drešević on "Crna Goro Najljepša" (Crna Goro Kapljo Vode... compilation,
2005).

NOT to be confused with Marimanga, the 1970s Kosovar rock band led by Njazi
Bytyqi — a separate artist (see artist "Marimanga").

## 3. Profile for 7654285 "Nijazi Bytyqi" (person — keep this page)

Albanian guitarist, singer and songwriter from Prizren, Kosovo (born
1946/1947, died 3 March 2018). Described in his koha.net obituary as "the
initiator of pop-rock music in Kosovo." Led the band Marimanga, one of the
first rock groups in Kosovo, whose best-known recording "Vogelushja Keti"
was cut in 1970 at TVP studios in Pristina. Later led the groups
Therandosët and Luniazët. In 1977 released two Turkish-language songs on
Jugoton (7", "Beni Unutma" / "Bir Sevgilim Var"). Credited with making what
is believed to be the first Albanian-language music video, filmed in Prizren
in the 1970s and broadcast on Croatian television. Withdrew from public life
after the 1970s but continued playing guitar until his death in 2018. His
sons Elvis and Gjulian Bytyqi, both guitarists, preserved his archive; Elvis
released the double LP "Nijazi Bytyqi" in 2024.
