# Marimanga (1970): The Spiders of Prizren

Nijazi Bytyqi was 24 when he walked into TVP studios in Pristina and recorded what might be the first Kosovar rock song ever committed to tape. 1970. He called his band Marimanga — Spiders.

He made the first Albanian-language music video in Yugoslavia — broadcast on Croatian national TV. Before him: nothing. After: a lineage to MAK, Gjurmët, every Kosovar band.

"Vogelushja Keti" survives on YouTube — 17K views. Two Turkish songs pressed on Jugoton vinyl in 1977. Then he stopped.

In February 2026, his sons Elvis and Gjulian uploaded 22 songs to streaming platforms. The music survived because his family made sure it did.

No English Wikipedia page. No academic paper naming the band. The songs are in the open. The story was missing.

Until now.
