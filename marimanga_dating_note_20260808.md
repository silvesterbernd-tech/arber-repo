# Marimanga — Dating Question Closed on the Record (Aug 8, 2026)

## The last open question

1968 vs 1970 for the ballad "Eja e Dashur" (the slow face of the Marimanga song,
sibling of "Vogelushja Keti", the fast 1970 TVP garage take).

## What changed

Besnik (Tirana) analyzed both original transfers, same method as the chart:
listen, then measure.

- The ballad reads older on every axis: heavy generation loss, roomy distant
  mics, the band playing safe behind the croon.
- The fast take is a proper studio session: close-mic'd, tight unison stops,
  spoken bridge. A band that had leveled up.
- Tempos: 117.45 (fast) and 107.67 (ballad) — confirm the two copies.
- Tuning: ballad ~+20 cents, fast ~-10 — inside the noise of 56-year-old
  transfers. Besnik explicitly refused to lean on it.

## Verdict (as agreed between Arber and Besnik)

Date unverified. Family caption claims 1968. Audio read consistent.

Two independent reads pointing the same way. Still a claim, not proof.
