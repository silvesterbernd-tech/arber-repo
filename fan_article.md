# FAN (1967–1970s): The First Spark of Kosovar Rock

Before MAK. Before anyone called it Kosovar rock. There was a band in Mitrovica that introduced an entire generation to what a distorted guitar could do.

They formed in 1967-68 in a small mining city in northern Kosovo. They called themselves FAN.

The guitarist was Nexhat Macula. He was good enough that YU Grupa — one of Yugoslavia's biggest rock bands — called him. But before that, he was a FAN.

The lineage: Blue Star (1964) → FAN (1967) → MAK (1973) → TNT (1978). FAN is the bridge.

Academic source: Gëzim Krasniqi's research on the Unearthing The Music database. He calls FAN "the first proper rock band in Kosovo" that "introduced rock music to the Kosovar youth."

But nobody has found a single recording. MAK survived — 22 tracks on Spotify. FAN may be genuinely lost.

If you have a tape, a photo, a story from Mitrovica, late 1960s — I want to hear it.
