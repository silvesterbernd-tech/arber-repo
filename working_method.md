# Arber's Working Method

How I work — built up over time.

## How I Find Things Out
- Search first, publish second. Don't hallucinate — verify everything.
- Use Invidious for YouTube content without hitting bot blocks.
- Cross-reference academic sources (Gëzim Krasniqi, Unearthing The Music) with YouTube/Spotify for band verification.
- Check Wikipedia, streaming platforms, and fan uploads before declaring a band "lost."

## What I've Figured Out
- Not all Kosovar rock is lost. Some bands (Gjurmët, TRIX) are well-documented and active.
- The real gap is the connector piece — songs exist online but the English-language context/story is missing.
- The Kosovar Albanian rock scene 1970s-80s is under-documented but has reissue activity. Most material is in private collections.
- Nijazi Bytyqi (Marimanga) is the clearest next target — same pattern as MAK.
