## Research Findings (Aug 1)
- FAN (Mitrovica, late 1960s) confirmed as Kosovo's first proper rock band
- Source: Gëzim Krasniqi's academic study on Unearthing The Music database
- Formed 1967-68 in Mitrovica
- Guitarist Nexhat Macula later played in YU Grupa and formed TNT (1978)
- Band lineage: Blue Star/Modestët (1964) → FAN (1967-68) → MAK (1973) → TNT (1978)

## UTM Database Research (Aug 3)
Full list of Kosovar rock bands documented in Gëzim Krasniqi study:
- Blue Stars / Modestët (1964, Prishtina) — First Albanian rock band in Kosovo
- FAN (late 1960s, Mitrovica) — Already documented
- Marimanga (1970) — Led by Njazi Bytyqi. Recorded at TVP studios. NEW DISCOVERY
- MAK (1973, Mitrovica) — Already documented
- TRIX (1974) — Veli Sahiti, multiple albums. Turns out documented.
- Gjurmët (1980-1986) — Most successful. 18k monthly listeners, Wikipedia page.
- Plus: Lindja, 403, Minatori, TNT, Ilirët, Fisnikët, Telex, Seleksioni 039, Menkis

## Day 6-7 (Aug 3-4)
- Gjurmët found to be well-documented (Wikipedia, Spotify).
- TRIX confirmed documented (English Wikipedia, 20 albums, active).
- Marimanga found: Nijazi Bytyqi (1946-2018, Prizren). TVP archive video on YouTube (17K views). Full 22-song catalog on Spotify/Apple Music (Feb 2026). No English Wikipedia. Sons Elvis and Gjulian Bytyqi preserved the archive.
- Nijazi Bytyqi started at Agimi in Prizren. Made first Albanian-language music video (1970s, broadcast on Croatian TV). Two Turkish songs on Jugoton vinyl 1977.
