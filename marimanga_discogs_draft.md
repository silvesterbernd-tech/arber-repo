## ARTIST SUBMISSION — MARIMANGA

**Name:** Marimanga
(also rendered Marimangat / Merimangat — Albanian for "the spider")

**Real Name:** (leave blank — band name, not pseudonym)

**Profile (paste-ready text):**

Albanian rock band from Kosovo, led by guitarist and singer Njazi Bytyqi
(1946/1947 – 3 March 2018, Prizren). One of the first rock groups in Kosovo,
appearing on the scene around 1970 per Gëzim Krasniqi's study of the Albanian
rock scene in Yugoslavia; a Facebook page connected to Bytyqi dates the group
to 1968, so the formation year is not settled.

The band's best-known recording is "Vogelushja Keti" ("Petite Keti"),
recorded in 1970 in the studios of TVP (Televizioni i Prishtinës), the
Pristina television station, and popular at the time. Session footage
survives on the RTP Arkiv YouTube channel ("Njazi Bytyqi Grupi Marimanga –
Arkiv 1970 TVP"); this appears to be the only surviving trace of the
recording. A first chart and Gheg-language lyric transcription of the song
was produced in 2026 (Besnik / Arber), the first documented transcription in
any language.

Bytyqi is described in his koha.net obituary (5 March 2018) as "the initiator
of pop-rock music in Kosovo"; bassist Sheki Hoxha called him "the first of
the first in Kosovo's pop & rock 'n' roll." Marimanga recorded in Pristina
and later in Croatia. Bytyqi made what is believed to be the first
Albanian-language music video, filmed in Prizren in the 1970s and broadcast
on Croatian television (the show "Nedeljom početje", hosted by Sasha
Zalepugin, Zagreb). In 1977 Bytyqi released two Turkish-language songs on
Jugoton (vinyl), including "Beni Unutma". He withdrew from public life after
the 1970s but continued playing guitar until his death in 2018. His sons
Elvis and Gjulian Bytyqi, both guitarists, preserved his archive; Elvis
released the double LP "Nijazi Bytyqi" in 2024.
